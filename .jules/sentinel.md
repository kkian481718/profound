## 2024-05-24 - [Medium] Prevent PII leakage to Third-Party Analytics Platforms
**Vulnerability:** Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as administrator email addresses and user physical addresses were being sent as plain text in payloads to Google Analytics (via `trackAdminEvent` and `trackOrderEvent`).
**Learning:** Even seemingly benign analytical event tracking can inadvertently capture and broadcast highly sensitive information. In this application, detailed location text strings combined with context could locate a specific user, and admin emails were exposed outright in the tracking logs. Data sent to third-party providers must be strictly sanitized.
**Prevention:** Ensure analytics payloads use generic placeholders (e.g. tracking "success" instead of the literal admin email string) or categorical data (e.g. generic pickup zones like "校內教學區" or "校外" instead of detailed delivery instructions). Always review what exact event labels and parameters are sent via frontend tracking scripts.
